About Project

Predictive Maintenance with NASA C-MAPSS

AeroLife estimates the Remaining Useful Life (RUL) of turbofan engines from operating settings and sensor readings.

Project Overview

The project uses the NASA Commercial Modular Aero-Propulsion System Simulation (C-MAPSS) dataset. Each record describes one engine at one cycle, with three operating settings and 21 sensor measurements. A regression model maps those inputs to the engine's remaining useful life in cycles.

  • Four subsets covering different operating conditions and fault modes
  • Engine-wise validation to avoid leaking the same engine across splits
  • Comparison of linear, tree-based, boosting and kernel models
  • Paired t-tests with Holm correction for model significance
  • Prediction intervals from historical validation errors

Evaluation

Models are scored with MAE, RMSE and R² on an engine-wise validation split. The selected model is also checked with grouped cross-validation, a near-failure MAE slice and paired t-tests against every alternative.

Important Note

These results come from simulated C-MAPSS engines. They are useful for comparing models and demonstrating a predictive-maintenance workflow, but they are not certified maintenance advice for real aircraft.
